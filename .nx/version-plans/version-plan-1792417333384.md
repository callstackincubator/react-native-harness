On Android 8 and later, `screen.screenshot()` captures what a TextureView or a SurfaceView shows, such as a GPU-rendered canvas, a video or a camera preview, instead of transparent pixels. An on-screen capture now copies what the window shows, so effects only the GPU renderer draws, such as elevation shadows and `filter` blurs, appear in it, and an Android baseline recorded without them needs recording again; a capture whose copy fails, or whose window is not shown, as while the screen is off, resolves `null` instead of a different image. An element under a scale or rotation transform is captured in the box it covers on the screen, as on iOS. An element partly off the screen, scaled to nothing or detached from the window is still drawn by itself, at its scroll offset, so a TextureView or a SurfaceView in it still comes out transparent; on Android 8.0 and 8.1 a window with elevation is drawn from its view tree, as before, with its SurfaceViews behind it; and a SurfaceView set above its window with `setZOrderOnTop` is still left out.
